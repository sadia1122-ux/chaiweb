Our Heritage

The Art of Punjabi tea culture

A Legacy of Gathering, Conversation, & Poetry

In the heart of Punjab, a "Mehfil" is not just a gathering—it is a celebration of life, connection, and deep-felt conversation, always anchored by a warm cup of tea. At Mehfil-e-Chai, we bring the royal grand atmosphere of traditional Punjabi tea-making into the modern age.

Every cup we brew utilizes ancestral techniques: slowly simmered black tea dust, hand-crushed cardamoms, organic cinnamon bark, and fresh dairy milk heated over slow copper stoves. We craft a sensory journey that brings you closer to our roots.

Why Mehfil-e-Chai

The Royal Difference

What sets us apart is our absolute dedication to authenticity, premium ingredients, and royal ambience.

Fresh Ingredients

We use daily-sourced farm milk, fresh organic ginger roots, and hand-ground green cardamom seeds. No artificial extracts, ever.

Authentic Punjabi Taste

Our recipes are ancestral heritage formulas passed down through generations, yielding the exact richness of old Lahori tea stands.

Hygienic Kitchen

We combine traditional slow-cooking methods with a state-of-the-art sterile kitchen workspace adhering to international food standards.

Fast Service

Our experts brew in large copper drums constantly, allowing us to serve you blazing hot, piping fresh tea with minimal wait times.

Premium Quality

From sourcing whole tea leaves from Assam and Kenya to choosing pure organic saffron threads, quality is our sacred trust.

Affordable Prices

We offer high-end royal café experience, warm customer service, and exquisite tastes at rates accessible to all tea lovers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions regarding our recipes, bookings, and operations.

We specialize in traditional Punjabi brews (like slow-cooked Doodh Patti and Karak Chai), earthy Kulhad Chai, aromatic spiced Masala/Elaichi teas, and the signature pink Kashmiri Chai. We also offer luxurious gourmet infusions, including genuine saffron, Belgian chocolate, and Madagascar vanilla-infused tea.

We are open daily from 7:00 AM to 1:00 AM. We serve breakfast combos from 7:00 AM to 11:30 AM, and our complete tea, snack, and dinner menus are available all day. We are located at 12-A, Gulberg Boulevard, Lahore, Pakistan.

We have strict supply-chain controls. Our whole-cream buffalo milk is delivered fresh every morning from local dairy farms, and our ginger, cardamom, and spice elements are freshly crushed by hand in our kitchens throughout the day. We never use artificial flavorings, powder mixtures, or preservatives.

Yes, you can reserve a table directly using our website contact form or by calling us. For home deliveries, we partner with leading food delivery apps (Foodpanda/Cheetay) and offer our own direct fast-delivery service within a 5km radius.
